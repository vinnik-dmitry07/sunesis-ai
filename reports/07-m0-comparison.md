4. **Original input sets differ by two inert cells.** `main` stores 68 cells, `valuation-range` 66. The extra two (`Input sheet!B49` = 0.15 and `Input sheet!B57` = 1) equal the workbook's own constants; B49 only matters when the terminal-return override is on.

7. **Reverse DCF.** From the original set both branches need the same parallel shift (-4.42 pp, to 4.58% fading to 3.58%). From their own Base, `main` needs -1.14 pp (6.36% to 5.86%) and `valuation-range` -1.98 pp (5.42% flat).
8. **Artifacts.** `main`'s workbook was never recalculated in Excel. `valuation-range`'s was. `main`'s `measurement.json` records a `sourceModelHash` that changes with working-tree line endings: `main` has no `.gitattributes`, so on this Windows checkout `index.html` gets CRLF endings and the hash differs from the committed one. `valuation-range`'s committed `snapshots/original.json` recorded the hash of an earlier `index.html`.
9. **Known error not yet fixed.** `valuation-range` divides every scenario's guided operating income by the Base revenue when it sets the year-1 margin, so Bear and Bull do not reproduce their own guidance. M1 fixes and measures it.
